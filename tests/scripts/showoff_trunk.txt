# layout, its places, each piece's own model and the world's stand-in boxes), then O opens the trunk and photo mode's
# orbit saves the page from above the trunk and from its two corners (up, left, right).  With
# NFSU2_TEST_TRUNK_GAMEWAY=1 the trunk is drawn the game's way (the stand-in boxes), for the before picture.
