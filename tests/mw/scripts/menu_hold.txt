# Most Wanted: the main menu held for 60 s of wall time, for the frame rate there and the front end's clocks (TRACKER
# M7-29).  Boots to the main menu as boot_menu.txt does.  The menu's camera swings in for 6 s of the game's real
# time (view 1's camera: the eView array 0x9195E0, 0x70 a view, its Camera at +0x40 and the camera's position at
# +0x40; 6.000 s of RealTimer at 60, 120 and 240 fps alike), so from the menu's first frame its position is traced
# every 2 frames with RealTimer [0x925B14] (1/4000 s) until 7 s of RealTimer have gone by.  Then a [status] line
# over the next 60 s (`status` prints one over the window since the line before it: fps, guest busy ms per frame
# p50/p95/p99/max, the guest lock and the pacer's spin), with RealTimeFrames [0x8F82F4] (1/60 s units) and
# RealTimer peeked at both ends, and the game closed by WM_CLOSE.  tests/mw/checks/menu_hold.sh wants the swing
# over 6 s after the menu came up both in wall time and in RealTimer (the front end's cameras on the wall clock at
# any frame rate: runtime/mw/framerate_mw.c), and RealTimeFrames at 60 per second of RealTimer over the hold
# (0x65C6D0 makes them at least one a frame, which framerate_mw.c undoes above 60).  NFSU2_FPS=off, the game's own
# limiter, fails both by design: its cameras get 0.01 s on a frame without a step, and RealTimeFrames one a frame.
#   node web/tools/run-suite.mjs --game mw --script menu_hold.txt --marker 'menu_hold: 60 s held' --env NFSU2_FPS=120
# On the renderer's test page (test/play.html, WebGPU) with the page's frame-rate setting (NFSU2_FPS):
#   .../test/play.html?arg=/data/speed.exe&script=/tests/scripts/menu_hold.txt&env=NFSU2_FPS=120&...
# (an oracle build also needs the D3DX seed of tests/mw/scripts/boot_menu.txt)
