# Most Wanted: the main menu held for 60 s of wall time, for the frame rate there (TRACKER M7-29).  Boots to the
# main menu as boot_menu.txt does, waits 10 s for the menu's camera swing and loads to settle, then takes a
# [status] line over the next 60 s (`status` prints one over the window since the line before it: fps, guest busy
# ms per frame p50/p95/p99/max, the guest lock and the pacer's spin), and closes the game by WM_CLOSE.  On the
# renderer's test page (test/play.html, WebGPU) with the page's frame-rate setting (NFSU2_FPS):
#   .../test/play.html?arg=/data/speed.exe&script=/tests/scripts/menu_hold.txt&env=NFSU2_FPS=120&...
# The peeks at both ends give the clocks' rates over the window: the front end's camera track time [0x925AB4]
# (0x64C2D0, s; the main menu's camera moves along it), RealTimeFrames [0x8F82F4] (1/60 s units, at least one
# a frame: 0x65C6D0) and RealTimer [0x925B14] (1/4000 s).
# (an oracle build also needs the D3DX seed of tests/mw/scripts/boot_menu.txt)
0     waitmem 0x00925e90 3 3600       # the front end (GameFlow 3)
+0    tapuntil RETURN MainMenu.fng 90 6000
+0    waitfng MainMenu.fng 600
+10000ms status menu_settle
+0    peek 0x00925ab4
+0    peek 0x008f82f4
+0    peek 0x00925b14
+60000ms status menu_hold60
+0    peek 0x00925ab4
+0    peek 0x008f82f4
+0    peek 0x00925b14
+0    log menu_hold: 60 s held
+0    close
