# Most Wanted: Custom Race drags on one track, from the load to the results screen, MWK_RUNS times in a row (TRACKER

# (0x136C5C90), RIGHT until the track's name label MWK_TRACK is up, the options as they come, the car the list
# starts on; a drag asks no transmission (no "Select your transmission" dialog comes).  The race is driven over the
# line: from GO the game's AI drives the player's car (autopilot, Game.ForceAIControl), its gear changes included
# (where logs the speed on the way: 60, 100 and 126 km/h at 5, 10 and 15 s in the first runs), until the race's end
# screen takes input: the winner's InGamePhotoMaster.fng (its "WINNER" label 0x5DFB6667 up) or PostRace_Results.fng
# (the player lost, or its car was out of the race: a drag ends a racer that crashes); racestatus says where the
# player came and whether it was finished.  Enter until the "quick race" menu is back.  No racewin: the race's own
# script finishes every racer.
#   node web/tools/run-suite.mjs --game mw --script race_drag.txt --marker 'race_drag: done' \
#     --env MWK_TRACK=4af3c8df --env MWK_RUNS=2 --env NFSU2_FNG_LOG=3 --env NFSU2_QUIT_FRAMES=100000 --timeout-scale 6
