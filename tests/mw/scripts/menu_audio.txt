#                       rate (a word) and [0x009c205c] +3 its output channels (a byte)
#   [sound] buffer ...  the DirectSound buffers it made (with SNDBUF=1 also the formats it asked for)
#   [mw-sound] ...      the sound thread's count of polls with a still cursor (runtime/mw/sound_mw.c; 100 rebuilds
#                       DirectSound)
#   [audio] ...         the mixer's status every NFSU2_AUDIO_STATUS seconds: mixed= against t= is the cursors' pace
#   generated guest code: ... calls, ... instructions   (no FAILED: genexec ran all of the sound banks' code)

#     --env MENU_MS=60000 --env NFSU2_AUDIO_STATUS=10
