# Most Wanted: what Speedbreaker (G, the GAMEBREAKER binding) does in a single-player Custom Race, the control for the
# LAN race test of TRACKER M8-22 (lan-pair --game mw, the host taps G with the AI let go).  One Custom Race of kind
# MWK_MODE on track MWK_TRACK (as race_kind.txt picks them); from GO the game's AI drives (autopilot on) for 600
# frames, lets go (autopilot off), G is tapped, and `where` logs the car's position and speed every 30 frames for
# 6 s: in slow motion the car covers less than its speed's worth of ground in the 30 frames.  Then the AI drives
# again, racewin ends the race and Enter goes back to the menu.
#   node web/tools/run-suite.mjs --game mw --script race_speedbreaker.txt --marker 'race_speedbreaker: done' \
#     --env MWK_MODE=4b4e388a --env MWK_TITLE=3de80a85 --env MWK_TRACK=4d36bf77 --env MWK_RUNS=1 \
#     --env NFSU2_FNG_LOG=3 --env NFSU2_QUIT_FRAMES=100000 --timeout-scale 6
