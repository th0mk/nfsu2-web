# Custom Race circuit (City Perimeter, 3 opponents, Traffic Level at Maximum), the game's AI driving the player's car
# from GO to the race's own end: the player's finished byte (racer slot 0, GRacerInfo +0x21 at [0x91E000]+0x39).
# racestatus then logs each racer's share and race time.  With NFSMW_HFR_TRACE=1 the [hfrtr] lines (every 0.25 s
# of game time) give the lap times (the player's share of the race crossing each lap's end), the racers, the
# traffic's stops and the Speedbreaker; NFSU2_HFR_FT=1 the frame times ([mwft], tools/mw/hfr_frames.py);
# NFSMW_HFR_TRAIL=1 the NOS trails ([hfrtrail], runtime/mw/hfr_mw.c).  A world steps at 60 unless
# NFSMW_WORLD_HFR=1 steps it at the page's rate:
#   node web/tools/run-suite.mjs --game mw --script hfr_laps.txt --marker 'hfr_laps: finished' \
#     --env NFSU2_FPS=120 --env NFSMW_WORLD_HFR=1 --env NFSMW_HFR_TRACE=1 --env NFSU2_HFR_FT=1 --timeout-scale 8
