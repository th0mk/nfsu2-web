# Most Wanted: the Quick Race options matrix (TRACKER M4-07).  Five Custom Races in one session, each set up on the
# screens and raced to the results: four circuits on the first track, City Perimeter, and a sprint on Seaside & Power

# Opponents 1, 2 and 3, Difficulty Easy, Medium and Hard, Catch Up On and Off, a circuit's laps 5, 4, 6 and 1 (laps that
# none of the opponents' counts in the same screen begin with, as the text waits match a string's first characters), and
# the cars the car list offers with no profile, the 1st, 2nd and 4th of its 32 (the list opens on the car taken last, so
# LEFT first goes back to its first: "1 / 32"; the 5th is locked, Enter does nothing there), the 2nd and 4th twice.
# Track_Options.fng's rows (0xB2980BA8 on, DOWN to the next): a circuit's Laps (1 to 8; its name label 0x48494E83 runs
# HIGHLIGHT on the first row), then Traffic Level, Opponents, Difficulty and Catch Up; a sprint starts at Traffic Level.
# LEFT steps a value back with wrap-around (a DOWN waits 12 frames after the last tap: a key tapped again while the last
# tap still holds it, 6 frames, makes no new press), so each value is waited for by its label (a count by its text) on

# the game reads them (laps, traffic level, difficulty, catch-up, length), its racers and the player's car

# and 3000 (the vehicles by driver class: traffic is class 1), then the shortcut finish (racewin) and the winner's

# d3dx9_26.dll alone).  tests/mw/checks/race_options.sh wants each race's parameters to be the ones set on its screens,
