      Changes from the original

      • The PC game's own codeRecompiled to run in Chrome and Firefox, with WebGPU for the graphics and Web Audio for the sound.

      • Up to 120 fpsWidescreen at your screen's resolution.

      • The original lookWith the game's graphics settings at their highest.

      • Startup movies skipWith one press.

      • Free roam & OnlineWith friends on this server, no EA account needed.

      • A hornTo honk at the other players, in free roam and in races.

      • Xbox & PlayStation controllersWith rumble.

      • Pauses in a background tabExcept in LAN and Online play.

      • Autosave for every profileAnd a note each time the game saves.

      • Saves stay in this browserWith zip backups.
