    The PC game's own code, recompiled for Chrome and Firefox: up to 120 fps in widescreen, free roam and Online races with friends, and controllers, wheels and touch controls.

    My brother and I played this game together about 20 years ago. Because of that I tried porting it to the browser so we could play together again. It worked surprisingly well, and it got a bit out of hand. It's great to see so many people playing it.

    To help with hosting and development costs, you can buy me a coffee. No perks, apart from a Discord role.

    Rights holders: legal@nfsu2.xyz. Support emails won't be answered; ask on our Discord.

    Source code on GitHub. Based on Talha Müderrisoğlu's NFSU2Recomp, the original x86 → C static recompiler for NFSU2, after N64: Recompiled and XenonRecomp. This project was forked from NFSU2Recomp's MIT-licensed version of 16 October 2026 and uses only code from that version.

    Texture packs: LunicAura106's Upscaled HUD, JeansBig's Detailed Map.

    Dubbed voices and movies, and Chinese, Japanese, Russian and Thai text: KyoriAsh's Multi 13 languages mod, with Lee-Ryo's Thai support.

    Portuguese (Brazil): TraduMix and MixMods (Fyro, Beater, Kerlyston F.; Junior_Djjr, SideWinder, Elyzandro).

    Polish: PLProjekt (Daniel Graszka, MaDeR, Marcin Piech, Nati, Mateusz Wilkowski, alter).

    Turkish: Samet Samyeli; fonts: muntazam.

    Hungarian: GameHunter (L_zooltan).

    Ukrainian: Clark X.
