    Changes from the original

    The PC game's own code, recompiled to run in Chrome and Firefox with WebGPU graphics and Web Audio sound. It runs at up to 120 fps in widescreen at your screen's resolution, with the original look at its highest settings.

    Drive with friends in free roam and race them in Online mode on this server, with no account, and honk at them with eight horns. Play on a keyboard, an Xbox or PlayStation controller, a steering wheel or a phone's touch controls, with your saves in this browser or in the cloud.

    About this project

    My brother and I played this game together about 20 years ago. I tried porting it to the browser, and it worked surprisingly well, so I put it online for others to enjoy. It got a bit out of hand. It's great to see so many people playing it.

    If you enjoy the project and want to help with the costs of hosting and development, you can buy me a coffee. It comes with no perks, apart from a role on the Discord server.

    Rights holders can email legal@nfsu2.xyz. Support emails won't be answered: for help, ask on our Discord.

    Credits

    The source code is on GitHub. This port is based on NFSU2Recomp by Talha Müderrisoğlu, the original x86 → C static recompiler for NFSU2, which in turn follows the method of N64: Recompiled and XenonRecomp.

    The language packs are built from KyoriAsh's Multi 13 languages mod (with Lee-Ryo's Thai support), from TraduMix's and MixMods' Brazilian Portuguese translation (Fyro, Beater, Kerlyston F.; Junior_Djjr, SideWinder, Elyzandro) and from PLProjekt's Polish translation (Daniel Graszka, MaDeR, Marcin Piech, Nati, Mateusz Wilkowski, alter). The texture pack is built from LunicAura106's Upscaled HUD.
